Interpretation

What the labels mean

Studied in Becker means at least one trial or clinical study includes patients with Becker, but does not imply efficacy. Becker plan / designation means an FDA orphan designation, explicit sponsor plan, or sponsor-defined indication; trials may still involve only Duchenne. DMD only / extrapolated means the treatment acts on a potentially shared pathological pathway, but no Becker efficacy evidence has been identified in the linked sources.

FDA orphan-drug designation does not mean the treatment is approved or that a clinical trial has enrolled patients. Trial listings and sponsor statements can change; check the linked primary documents before relying on a status.

Editorial process

Inclusion and sourcing

  • Included: direct Becker trials, explicit Becker programs, therapies acting on shared muscle degeneration/regeneration or dystrophin-associated cardiomyopathy.
  • Excluded: Duchenne-centered microdystrophin replacement and exon skipping without a specific Becker rationale.
  • Each therapy links to its developer or study. Each substantive factual statement has one or more numbered source links. Source cards identify the publisher and date.
  • Completed and discontinued programs remain visible so negative results are not lost. “No Becker trial identified” means not located in the cited evidence, not proof that none exists.
  • This is a curated, non-exhaustive snapshot; publication updates require independent editorial review. It is not clinical guidance.
